Outro Text Generator

An outro text generator creates polished YouTube outro text for your videos — your channel name, a tagline like "Thanks for watching!", and a call to action. What is outro text?

Outro text is the closing message of a video — the "thanks for watching" card, channel name, and call to action (like, subscribe, comment) that appears at the end. Creators use it to convert casual viewers into subscribers: the last thing someone sees is the easiest thing to act on. Good outro text is short, on-brand, and always includes one clear next step.

How this outro text generator works

Type your channel name and tagline (the default is "Thanks for watching!") and choose one of four style cards. Minimal is a dark, clean card with white type. Gaming uses a bold red gradient with hard shadow type. Elegant pairs a blue gradient with gold lettering. Neon glows cyan on near-black. The preview updates live as you type, showing exactly how the end card reads.

Below the preview, the tool generates four copy-paste text outros from the same inputs: Minimal (✦ NAME ✦ + tagline + like/subscribe/comment line), Fancy Box (your name in a ╔═╗ box-drawing frame), Stars (star-divided banner), and Gaming (▶ ▶ ▶ arrows with a gaming CTA line). These are plain-text designs with emoji and box-drawing characters — paste them into video descriptions, pinned comments, or community posts where the visual card can't go.

How to use this tool

  1. Type your channel name and tagline into the fields above.
  2. Pick a style card — Minimal, Gaming, Elegant, or Neon — and preview it live.
  3. Copy a text outro (Minimal, Fancy Box, Stars, or Gaming) with its copy button.
  4. Paste it into your video description, pinned comment, or community post.

What makes an outro actually work

The highest-converting outros share three traits. One clear action: "subscribe" beats a menu of five requests — pick the single behavior that matters most and lead with it. Brevity: viewers decide in about five seconds whether to stay for the end screen; your name, one tagline, one CTA is the whole formula. If you just need simple thanks for watching text, the Minimal style with the default tagline is enough. Consistency: using the same outro text and style across videos trains viewers to recognize the cue. Match the style to your niche — Gaming for gaming channels, Elegant for lifestyle or education, Neon for tech, Minimal when in doubt.

Limitations

Honest scope: this tool designs text and styled preview cards, not rendered video files — it won't export an MP4 end screen; for that, recreate the design in your video editor. The four styles are fixed presets; custom brand colors mean editing the concept yourself. The text outros rely on box-drawing characters and emoji, which render well on YouTube, Discord, and most phones but can misalign in apps with unusual fonts. Keep channel names reasonably short — long names overflow the Fancy Box frame.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I put in my YouTube outro?

Your channel name, a short tagline like "Thanks for watching!", and one clear call to action — usually subscribe. Type these into the generator above and it formats them into four styled outros instantly.

What are the best outro texts for videos?

The best outros are short and consistent: name + tagline + single CTA, in a style matching your niche. This tool's four presets (Minimal, Gaming, Elegant, Neon) cover the most common channel aesthetics.

Can I use the text outros in my video description?

Yes — that's exactly what they're for. The Minimal, Fancy Box, Stars, and Gaming outputs are plain Unicode text with emoji, so they paste cleanly into YouTube descriptions, pinned comments, and community posts.

Does this tool make video end screens?

No. It creates styled preview cards and copy-paste text outros, not MP4 files. Use the text and style as your design reference and build the actual end screen in your video editor.

Which outro style should I choose?

Match your niche: Gaming for gaming channels, Elegant for lifestyle or education, Neon for tech, Minimal as the safe default. Whichever you pick, use it consistently so viewers learn the cue.

How long should video ending text be?

Very short — viewers decide in seconds. Channel name, one tagline, one call to action. Anything more gets skipped, which is why all four of this tool's outputs follow that exact formula.

Will the box-drawing frames display correctly everywhere?

On YouTube, Discord, and modern phones, yes. Some apps with unusual fonts misalign box characters (╔ ═ ╝), so preview the Fancy Box outro where you'll post it before relying on it.

Where can I find YouTube outro ideas?

Right here: this tool's four presets are four ready-made YouTube outro ideas — Minimal for clean channels, Gaming for gaming content, Elegant for lifestyle or education, and Neon for tech. Type your channel name into each and pick the one that fits.

Is my channel name uploaded anywhere?

No. The preview and all text outros are generated in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
