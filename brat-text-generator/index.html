What Is Brat Text?

Brat text is the visual style of Charli XCX's 2024 album brat: lowercase, bold, slightly stretched sans-serif type centered on a flat lime-green square. The deliberately plain, low-effort look became a meme template — "brat green" edits flooded TikTok and X in the summer of 2024.

Unlike Unicode text generators, brat text here is rendered on a canvas and exported as an image file. That means the styling is pixel-perfect and identical on every device — but you can't paste it into a bio as text. Use this generator when you want the meme image itself: reaction pictures, fake album covers, story posts, and profile pictures.

How to Make Brat Text

  1. Type your text in the Main Text box. Use Enter for stacked lines, like the album cover.
  2. Pick a font. Montserrat (default) is closest to the cover look; Arial Black and Impact go bolder.
  3. Set colors. Keep the lime-green background for the authentic brat green text look, or change both colors freely.
  4. Choose a background mode — solid, a two-color gradient, or your own uploaded image.
  5. Adjust style and alignment with the Bold/Italic/Underline toggles and L/C/R buttons.
  6. Size the canvas with the width, height, and padding fields.
  7. Export as PNG or SVG — or use the 600×315 / 1200×630 presets for social posts.

What Font Is the Brat Album Cover?

The official brat cover uses a plain, slightly pixelated bold sans set in all lowercase on lime green. The generator's closest matches are Montserrat (the default) and Arial Black — both give you that blunt, unstyled attitude. Why is brat text lowercase? The lowercase setting is part of the aesthetic: it reads casual, deadpan, and anti-glamour, which is exactly the album's visual joke.

Tips for Authentic Brat Memes

Keep the text short and deadpan. The meme works because the design is aggressively plain — one to four lowercase words hit hardest.

Use the 1200×630 preset for X/Twitter and link previews; 600×315 is a lighter version of the same 1.91:1 social-card ratio.

Try SVG for crisp scaling. The SVG export stays sharp at any size, handy if you're printing stickers or enlarging the meme.

Limitations

  • The output is an image file, not text — you cannot paste "brat text" styling into bios, usernames, or chats.
  • Custom uploaded background images stay in your browser; the tool doesn't host or share them.
  • Fonts render from your system's font stack, so the exact look can vary slightly between devices.
  • The generator recreates the style of the album art for personal/fan use — it isn't affiliated with the artist or label.

Frequently Asked Questions

What font is the brat album cover?

The brat cover uses a plain bold sans-serif set in all lowercase on a lime-green square. In this generator, Montserrat (the default) and Arial Black come closest to that blunt, unstyled look.

How to make brat text?

Type your words into the generator, keep them lowercase, pick a bold sans font like Montserrat, leave the lime-green background, and click Download PNG. For stacked lines like the album cover, press Enter between words.

Why is brat text lowercase?

Lowercase is part of the album's anti-glamour aesthetic — casual, deadpan, and deliberately unpolished. The generator defaults to whatever case you type, so type in lowercase for the authentic look.

Can I copy and paste brat text into my bio?

Not as styled text — this tool exports images (PNG/SVG), and the brat look only exists as a picture. For text you can paste into bios, use a Unicode tool like the aesthetic text generator or vaporwave text generator instead.

What sizes can I export?

You can export SVG, a PNG at your custom canvas size, or one-click presets at 600×315 and 1200×630 pixels — the standard social-card ratio used by X, Facebook, and link previews.

Can I use my own background image?

Yes. Switch the background mode to Image and upload any picture from your device. The image stays in your browser — nothing is uploaded to a server.

Is there a faster way to download?

Yes — press Ctrl+S (Windows) or Cmd+S (Mac) while on the page to download the current canvas as a PNG instantly.

Is this generator affiliated with Charli XCX?

No. It's an unofficial fan-style tool that recreates the general look of the brat artwork for personal memes and edits.
