🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority

Last Updated: December 2025
✅ TL;DR: We don't collect your text. All processing happens in your browser. We don't require accounts or personal information. Your data stays with you.

1. Introduction

TextGen Tools is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our website and text generation tools.

2. Information We Don't Collect

We want to be clear about what we do NOT collect:

  • Your Text: All text you enter into our tools is processed entirely in your browser. We never see, store, or transmit your text to our servers.
  • Personal Information: We don't require registration. We don't ask for your name, email, or any personal details to use our tools.
  • Passwords: We have no login system, so no passwords are ever collected.
  • Payment Information: All our tools are free. We don't collect any payment details.

3. Information We May Collect

3.1 Automatically Collected Information

Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring website
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • General geographic location (country/region level)

This information is collected anonymously and used only to improve our services.

3.2 Cookies

We may use cookies for:

  • Remembering your preferences (like theme settings)
  • Analytics to understand how our tools are used
  • Advertising purposes (through third-party services)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

4. Third-Party Services

4.1 Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

4.2 Google AdSense

We display advertisements through Google AdSense to support our free services. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can manage your ad preferences at Google Ads Settings.

5. How We Use Information

Any information we collect is used to:

  • Improve and optimize our tools and website
  • Understand which tools are most popular
  • Ensure the website functions properly
  • Display relevant advertisements

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information:

  • Our website uses HTTPS encryption
  • Text processing occurs client-side (in your browser)
  • We don't store user-generated content on our servers

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of analytics tracking
  • Disable cookies in your browser

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Website after changes constitutes acceptance.
