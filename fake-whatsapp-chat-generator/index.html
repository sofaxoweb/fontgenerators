Fake WhatsApp Chat Generator

A fake WhatsApp chat generator builds a realistic-looking WhatsApp conversation mockup in your browser — contact name, online status, message bubbles with timestamps, and the familiar chat wallpaper. What is a fake WhatsApp chat generator?

A fake WhatsApp chat generator is a tool that draws a mockup of a WhatsApp conversation that looks like a phone screenshot. You supply the contact name, status text, and every message; the tool renders them in WhatsApp's visual style — the green-tinted header, the "Today" date divider, tail-shaped bubbles with small timestamps. It creates an image-style prop, not a real chat.

How this fake WhatsApp chat generator works

The page renders a WhatsApp-style chat window. In the header you set the contact name (the avatar circle shows its first letter) and the status line ("online", "last seen…", or anything you type). Below, a "Today" date divider anchors the thread. You type each message, set its time (e.g. 10:42), choose Sent (right-aligned, light green) or Received (left-aligned, white), and add it — the bubble appears instantly with its timestamp tucked in the corner. Clicking a bubble deletes it.

To be fully transparent about saving: the download button on this tool displays an alert instructing you to take a screenshot of the preview — there is no automatic PNG export here, so use your device's screenshot shortcut and crop to the chat. Like any WhatsApp chat maker, it only draws the mockup — nothing is sent anywhere; the "conversation" exists only as pixels in your browser.

Good uses for WhatsApp chat mockups

Content creators use these for comedy skits and "texting story" videos, marketers mock up testimonial-style chats for ads, designers placeholder real-looking threads in app presentations, and friends prank each other with absurd invented conversations. Prefer the iPhone look instead? The fake message generator builds the same kind of prop in iMessage style with blue and gray bubbles.

Limitations

Honest limits. This is a visual approximation of WhatsApp's interface, not a pixel-perfect clone — close inspection reveals differences in fonts and spacing. There is no automatic image export; you screenshot manually. Messages are plain text with timestamps — no voice notes, no real double-tick read receipts, no link previews, no encryption indicators. The status line and times are free text you invent, and nothing here connects to WhatsApp or sends messages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to create fake WhatsApp chat?

Enter a contact name and status in the tool above, then add messages one by one — choosing Sent or Received and setting each timestamp. Screenshot the preview to save your mockup.

Can I download the fake WhatsApp conversation as an image?

This tool doesn't auto-export images — use your device's screenshot shortcut on the preview, then crop to the chat. The download button shows these instructions when clicked.

Does this send real WhatsApp messages?

No. It only draws a mockup in your browser. No messages are sent, no phone numbers are used, and nothing connects to WhatsApp.

Can I set custom times on each message?

Yes. Each message has its own time field (e.g. 10:42) shown in the bubble's corner, plus the header status is free text like "online" or "last seen today".

How do I delete a message from the mockup?

Click any message bubble to remove it immediately. A clear button wipes the entire thread if you want to start over.

Is there an iPhone message version?

Yes — our fake message generator makes the same kind of mockup in iPhone iMessage style with blue sent and gray received bubbles.

Will people be able to tell it's fake?

At a glance it looks convincing, but it's an approximation — fonts, spacing, and details differ from the real app under close inspection. That's actually a good thing: keep pranks obviously harmless.

Is making fake chats legal?

As jokes, video props, or design mockups, yes. Using fabricated chats to deceive, defame, or commit fraud is not. Never present a mockup as genuine evidence of a conversation.

Do I need to sign up?

No. It's free, needs no account, and everything runs in your browser — your mockup never leaves your device.
