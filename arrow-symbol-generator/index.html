Arrow Symbol Generator

An arrow symbol generator gives you every arrow worth pasting — 151 symbols across simple, decorative, double, and curved styles, plus arrow emojis. Click any → arrow for instant arrow symbols copy paste — perfect for bios, documents, designs, and messages.

Key takeaways

  • 151 arrow symbols in 5 groups: simple, decorative, double, curved, and arrow emojis.
  • Simple arrows (→ ← ↑ ↓) come from Unicode's Arrows block (U+2190–U+21FF).
  • One click copies any arrow — no typing of Alt codes or character maps.
  • Arrows are plain text: they paste into Word, Google Docs, bios, code, and chat.
  • For pixel-identical rendering everywhere, prefer text arrows over arrow emojis.

What is an arrow symbol generator?

Definition: An arrow symbol generator is a free online tool that lists copy-paste arrow symbols — from the basic → (U+2192) to decorative ➔, double-headed ⟷, and curved ↻ arrows — organized by style so you can find and copy the exact arrow you need.

Arrows are workhorses: they point, they separate, they decorate. Designers use them in dividers, writers use them for "see also" pointers, and bio stylists use cute aesthetic arrows (⇢ ➳) as bullets. Memorizing their Alt codes is nobody's idea of fun — hence this page.

How this tool works

The page renders five grids from built-in lists: simple arrows (30: → ← ↑ ↓ ↔ ↕ plus diagonals and returns), decorative arrows (38: ➔ ➜ ➤ ➢ and other Dingbats-style pointers), double arrows (34: ⟵ ⟶ ⟷ ⟸ ⟹ for math and logic), curved arrows (28: ↻ ↺ ⤾ ⤿ for refresh and rotation), and arrow emojis (21 color pictographs). Clicking any symbol writes it to your clipboard.

The text arrows live mainly in three Unicode blocks: Arrows (U+2190–U+21FF) for → ← ↑ ↓, Supplemental Arrows-A/B for ⟵ ⟶ ⤀, and Dingbats (U+2700–U+27BF) for ➔ ➤ ➢. The Unicode Consortium has encoded arrows since version 1.1, so support is near-universal — even minimalist fonts include →.

Worked example

You are formatting a résumé bullet list and want something sharper than a hyphen. Scroll to Decorative Arrows, click ➔ — it copies. Paste before each item:

➔ Led a team of six engineers
➔ Shipped three major releases

Need a "back to top" marker for a long document? The Curved Arrows group has ⤴ and ↻ — one click, one paste.

Limitations

  • Arrow emojis vary by device. Text arrows (→ ➔ ⟷) render identically everywhere; color arrow emojis depend on the OS emoji font.
  • Some decorative arrows need good font coverage. Supplemental Arrows-B glyphs (⤀ ⤁) can show as boxes on very old systems.
  • Direction in RTL text. In right-to-left layouts, → still points right — it does not mirror automatically.
  • Screen readers name them. "Rightwards arrow" announced mid-sentence can confuse; keep decorative arrows out of essential prose.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy and paste arrow symbols?

Click any arrow on this page — it copies to your clipboard instantly. Then paste it into your document, bio, chat, or design. All 151 arrows are plain Unicode text.

How do I type a → arrow on my keyboard?

On Windows, hold Alt and type 26 on the numpad. On Mac, press Control + Command + Space and search "arrow". In HTML, use &rarr;. Or skip all that and copy → from this page.

What are the different types of arrow symbols?

This library groups them into simple (→ ← ↑ ↓), decorative (➔ ➤), double (⟵ ⟶ ⟷), curved (↻ ↺), and emoji arrows — 151 symbols in total, each clickable to copy.

What is the Unicode for the right arrow →?

→ is U+2192 RIGHTWARDS ARROW in Unicode's Arrows block (U+2190–U+21FF). In HTML you can write it as &#8594; or &rarr;.

Can I use arrows in my Instagram bio?

Yes. Text arrows paste into Instagram bios, TikTok bios, and X display names without issues, and they render identically on iOS and Android — a safe aesthetic choice.

Do arrow symbols work in Word and Google Docs?

Yes — paste them like any character. In Word you can also use Insert → Symbol, but copying from here is faster. Arrows scale with your font size and print cleanly.

What are cute arrows for bios?

Why is my arrow showing as a box?

Boxes mean the active font lacks that glyph — most common with Supplemental Arrows-B characters on old systems. Switch to a basic arrow like → or update the OS fonts.
