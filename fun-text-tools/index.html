What Are Fun Text Tools?

Fun text tools are the playful corner of this site: glitch and matrix text effects, fake chat screenshots for pranks, animated text for posts and profiles, video outro text — and an Arabic calligraphy generator that transliterates Latin letters into Arabic script. They exist for entertainment: jokes with friends, eye-catching posts, video production, and creative experiments.

Like everything here, they are free, run in your browser, and need no account. One request: keep the fake chats as jokes. A fake message screenshot is funny when everyone knows it is fake — it is not a tool for deceiving people, forging evidence, or scamming anyone.

  • Fake message generator — builds realistic-looking fake iMessage and SMS conversation screenshots for pranks.

How to Use These Tools

  1. Pick your effect: a fake chat, a glitch or matrix style, an animation, or Arabic calligraphy.
  2. Enter your text (or, for fake chats, the conversation lines and names).
  3. Copy the result, download the screenshot, or grab the embed code for animations.
  4. Share it — and label prank screenshots as jokes when you post them.

Fake Chats: Funny Prank, Serious Responsibility

The fake message generator and fake WhatsApp chat generator recreate chat interfaces convincingly — that is the point, and also the risk. Use them for obvious jokes among friends who are in on it. Do not use them to fabricate conversations as evidence, to impersonate someone, or to trick people into believing something false. If you post a screenshot publicly, say it is fake in the caption. Funny stays funny only when nobody is misled.

The Arabic Calligraphy Generator, Explained

This tool fixes a common frustration: you want Arabic-script text but only have a Latin keyboard. Its transliteration mode converts Latin letters to Arabic phonetically — type “salam” and get سلام. It also understands the chat-Arabic digit conventions (3 for ع, 7 for ح, 5 for خ). This is a phonetic approximation, not a translation — it renders how words sound, not what they mean. Switch to styling mode to render Arabic-script input in decorative calligraphic fonts for designs, invitations, and social posts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I make a fake text message screenshot?

Open the fake message generator, enter the contact name and the message lines, and it renders a realistic iMessage or SMS screenshot you can download. Use the fake WhatsApp chat generator for WhatsApp-style conversations. Keep it as a clearly labeled joke — never present it as real.

What is glitch text and how is it made?

Glitch text is the distorted, corrupted-looking style popular in cyberpunk designs. Our glitch effect text generator builds it from Unicode characters and combining marks, while the matrix text generator renders animated falling character streams in the style of the Matrix films. Both are plain text or CSS — no images needed.

How does the Arabic calligraphy generator convert English to Arabic?

It transliterates phonetically: type Latin letters and it maps them to the Arabic-script letters that sound the same — “salam” becomes سلام. It also handles the 3/7/5 digit conventions from chat Arabic. Note this is pronunciation mapping, not translation, and a second mode styles Arabic-script text in calligraphic fonts.

Can I use animated text on my website?

Yes. The animated text generator and typing effect text generator produce animations you can use on web pages — the typing effect tool provides code you can embed. Keep animations subtle on content pages: heavy motion distracts readers and can bother visitors sensitive to movement.

Do glitch and matrix text work on Discord?

Glitch text made of Unicode characters pastes into Discord messages and usernames as ordinary text. The matrix rain effect is an animation, so it cannot be pasted as text — share it as a video or embed it on a page instead.

Are these fun text tools free?

Yes — all 8 are free with no limits and no account required. Everything is generated in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded or stored.
