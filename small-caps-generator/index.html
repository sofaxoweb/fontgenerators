What Is a Small Caps Generator?

A small caps generator converts lowercase text into small capital letters — petite caps that read as uppercase but sit at lowercase height. Print designers have used true small caps for centuries in headings and book titles. This tool recreates the look with Unicode characters (sᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘs), so you can use it anywhere: no word processor, no font settings, just copy and paste.

How This Tool Works

Type New Post and the base style returns ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ. Look closely: the final "s" stays lowercase. That's not a bug — Unicode simply has no small capital S in the blocks this tool draws from (phonetic extensions and modifiers), so unmapped letters pass through unchanged. The decorated variants frame the result: Dot Small Caps (• ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ •), Dash Small Caps (— ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ —), and Diamond Small Caps (❖ ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ ❖). Test your exact words first, since which letters convert depends on the alphabet's coverage.

Worked Example: An Aesthetic Bio Heading

Small caps are the signature look of minimalist Instagram bios. Type about me and choose Small Caps: ᴀʙᴏᴜᴛ ᴍᴇ — every letter converts here, giving a clean, even heading. Copy it above the plain-text details in your bio. For a post title with more flair, Dot Small Caps frames it: • ᴀʙᴏᴜᴛ ᴍᴇ •. The effect is subtle and refined — emphasis without shouting, which is exactly why the aesthetic community favors it over bold.

Small Caps vs ALL CAPS

Regular ALL CAPS reads as shouting and fills the full cap height; small caps keep the texture of lowercase text while adding formality. That's why book publishers set chapter openings and acronyms in small caps — distinction without aggression. In a bio, "ᴀʙᴏᴜᴛ ᴍᴇ" feels curated where "ABOUT ME" feels loud. Use small caps when you want a heading to feel designed rather than emphasized.

The Missing-Letters Quirk

The honest limitation of Unicode small caps: coverage is incomplete. Depending on the source block, letters like S, and sometimes others, have no small capital form. "New Post" becomes ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ — with a lowercase s sitting among capitals. Some converters substitute lookalikes; this one passes the letter through unchanged, which is the truthful behavior. If your word contains an unmapped letter, try rewording, or embrace it — most readers never notice.

Limitations

These are Unicode approximations, not typographic small caps: a real font's small caps match its design; these are borrowed from phonetic alphabets and can look slightly off next to each other. Screen readers may announce them oddly. And like all styled Unicode, small caps won't match plain-text search — keep searchable content normal. For print or professional documents, use your software's true small-caps feature instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a small caps generator?

A small caps generator converts your text into small capital letters — petite caps at lowercase height — using Unicode characters. It recreates the classic print heading style anywhere, with no word processor or font settings needed.

How do I make small caps text?

Type your text, review the conversion, pick plain Small Caps or a framed variant, click Copy, and paste it into your bio, heading, or post. Lowercase input converts most cleanly.

Why do some letters stay lowercase in small caps?

Unicode's small capital coverage is incomplete — some letters, like S, have no small-cap form in the source blocks, so they pass through unchanged. 'New Post' becomes ɴᴇᴡ ᴘᴏsᴛ. It is a limitation of the standard, not an error.

Small caps vs all caps — what's the difference?

Small caps keep lowercase texture and feel refined and designed; ALL CAPS fills the full cap height and reads as shouting. In bios, 'ᴀʙᴏᴜᴛ ᴍᴇ' feels curated where 'ABOUT ME' feels loud.

Are these real typographic small caps?

These are Unicode approximations borrowed from phonetic alphabets — close, but not a perfect typographic match. For print or professional documents, use your software's true small-caps feature, which is designed for the font.

Are small caps accessible?

Screen readers may announce small-cap letters unexpectedly, and converted text won't match plain-text search. Keep important or searchable content in normal text, and use small caps for decorative headings.

Can I use small caps in my Instagram bio?

Yes — small caps are the signature minimalist bio heading. They add structure and style without the visual noise of bold, and they render on virtually every device since the characters are decades-old Unicode.

What are the Dot, Dash, and Diamond styles?

Plain Small Caps is the most minimal and compatible. Dot, Dash, and Diamond variants add framing symbols for post titles and decorative headings. All four convert letters the same way — only the frame differs.
