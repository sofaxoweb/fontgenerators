What Are Small Caps?

Small caps (small capital letters) are letterforms that look like capital letters but are sized closer to lowercase letters in height. In professional typography, true small caps are specially designed glyphs at around 70–80% of full capital size. In Unicode, a set of modifier small capital letters (ᴀ ʙ ᴄ ᴅ) provides a digital equivalent that works as copy-pasteable characters. These characters come from the IPA (International Phonetic Alphabet) and Latin Extended blocks and are supported on virtually all modern devices.

Where Small Caps Text Is Used

Small caps carry an elegance that full uppercase and standard lowercase lack. In book typography, small caps appear in chapter headers, author bylines, and running heads. Fashion brands like Céline and The Row use small caps in their branding for a minimal, sophisticated look. LinkedIn professionals add small caps to their headline for a distinctive display name. Interior designers and architects use small caps in portfolio descriptions for a refined, high-end feel. The style communicates confidence and restraint — qualities that resonate in professional and creative contexts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why doesn't every letter convert to small caps?

Not all lowercase letters have a corresponding small capital character in Unicode. Letters like 'q' and 'x' don't have widely supported small cap equivalents, so they may display in their standard form or as a close approximation.

Do small caps work on Instagram?

Yes. Instagram displays Unicode modifier letter small capitals correctly in bios and captions on both iOS and Android.

Are small caps the same as lowercase?

No. Small caps look like miniature uppercase letters — they have the form of capital letters but are scaled to roughly the height of lowercase. They're distinct from both regular capitals and regular lowercase.

Can I use small caps for a professional LinkedIn headline?

Yes — many professionals use small caps in their LinkedIn headline and name field for a distinctive, editorial look. It's visible in search results and makes profiles more memorable.

Do small caps affect readability?

For short text (names, titles, headlines), small caps are very readable and elegant. For long-form body text, they slow reading slightly compared to mixed-case text. Best used sparingly for emphasis or headers.
