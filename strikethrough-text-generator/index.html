What Is Strikethrough Text?

Strikethrough text uses the Unicode Combining Long Stroke Overlay character (U+0336) placed after each letter, creating the appearance of a horizontal line through the text. It's different from HTML's <s> tag or markdown's ~~text~~ — those require platform support and don't travel with the text. Unicode strikethrough characters are embedded in the text itself, so they display everywhere unicode is rendered. The visual effect communicates "this was said / this was the plan" while showing it's been revised or reconsidered.

Creative Uses of Strikethrough Text

Strikethrough communicates multiple emotional tones. In humor, "I definitely d̶i̶d̶n̶'̶t̶ ate the whole cake" signals self-aware comedy. In social commentary, crossed-out words signal what was almost said — often more powerful than saying it directly. In technical writing, strikethrough marks deprecated options or old values that have been replaced. In to-do lists shared in messaging apps, crossing out completed items is satisfying and clear. In product descriptions, a crossed-out old price (̶$̶9̶9̶) next to the new price ($49) draws attention to the discount.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does strikethrough text work on Instagram?

Yes. Unicode strikethrough characters display correctly in Instagram bios, captions, and comments because they are actual text characters, not formatting codes.

Can I combine strikethrough with bold or italic?

Yes. Apply bold or italic first, then add strikethrough — or paste bold unicode and add combining strikethrough characters. The effects layer visually.

Is there a way to strikethrough in WhatsApp natively?

WhatsApp supports its own strikethrough formatting: wrap text in tildes (~like this~) for strikethrough within the app. For copy-paste strikethrough that works everywhere, use unicode characters from this tool.

Why does strikethrough look different on some devices?

Rendering of combining unicode characters varies by font. Most modern system fonts render strikethrough correctly, but some older or specialized fonts may display it at a slightly different vertical position.

Can I use strikethrough for SEO content?

Use HTML <s> or <del> tags for web page content (they have semantic meaning for screen readers). Unicode strikethrough is better for social media posts where HTML isn't available.
