Strikethrough Text Generator

What Is a Strikethrough Text Generator?

A strikethrough text generator is a tool that draws a line through your text using Unicode combining characters — marks that overlay each letter. Word processors have a strikethrough button; social apps and chats almost never do. This tool creates crossed-out text as plain characters, so you can paste s̶t̶r̶i̶k̶e̶t̶h̶r̶o̶u̶g̶h̶ into tweets, bios, Discord messages, and comments exactly as you see it here.

How This Tool Works

The classic style appends U+0336 (combining long stroke overlay) to every letter, so mistake becomes m̶i̶s̶t̶a̶k̶e̶. Slash Through uses U+0337 (combining short solidus overlay) for a diagonal cross: o̷o̷p̷s̷. The two decorated styles frame the crossed text: X Crossed (✗ m̶i̶s̶t̶a̶k̶e̶ ✗) and Bracket Cross ([m̶i̶s̶t̶a̶k̶e̶]). Because each letter carries its own strike mark, the line stays continuous across the whole word on modern renderers. That's the full recipe for crossed out text: one combining mark per letter, applied instantly.

Worked Example: The Playful Correction

Strikethrough's most beloved use is the visible self-correction: "I totally love̶ hate̶ Mondays." Type hate, choose Strikethrough, and you get h̶a̶t̶e̶ — paste it mid-sentence and readers see both the original thought and the joke. For a "sold out" label on a post, X Crossed adds framing: ✗ s̶o̶l̶d̶ o̶u̶t̶ ✗. For to-do lists in plain-text notes, plain Strikethrough marks done items without deleting them.

How to Cross Out Text

  1. Type the word or phrase in the box above.
  2. Pick a style — Strikethrough for classic, Slash for diagonal, X/Bracket for framed.
  3. Click Copy on your chosen style.
  4. Paste into your post, bio, message, or comment.

Where Strikethrough Shines

Beyond jokes, crossed-out text does real work. Sellers mark "s̶o̶l̶d̶ o̶u̶t̶" items without rewriting listings. Writers show edits transparently in shared drafts. Gamers strike through completed objectives in plain-text guides. Teachers mark common wrong answers in study notes. In each case the value is the same: the original stays readable under the line, so the correction carries its own context — something deletion can't do.

Strikethrough and Character Counts

Like its underline sibling, each strike mark is a real character: "mistake" (7 letters) becomes 14 characters with U+0336 applied. Keep crossed phrases short on limited platforms. One more quirk: because the strike is drawn per-letter, very long words can show tiny gaps between marks on some renderers. Short words look cleanest.

Limitations

Crossed-out Unicode inherits the usual combining-mark caveats: screen readers may spell out the marks, some platforms strip them (leaving plain text), and each mark doubles the character cost. It also won't match plain-text search. And a note on tone: strikethrough reads as playful or informal — don't use it for serious corrections in professional documents, where your editor's real strikethrough (or simply rewriting) is the right call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a strikethrough text generator?

A strikethrough text generator draws a line through your words using Unicode combining marks — characters that overlay each letter. The crossed-out text is plain characters, so it pastes into posts, bios, and chats where no strikethrough button exists.

How do I cross out text to copy and paste?

Type your word, choose the Strikethrough style, click Copy, and paste it. Each letter gets a U+0336 combining mark that renders as a continuous line through the word on modern devices.

Is strikethrough text good for jokes?

Yes — strikethrough is the classic tone marker for playful corrections ('I love̶ hate Mondays'), sarcasm, and showing your work. It reads as humorous and informal, which is exactly the effect in casual posts and chats.

What's the difference between the 4 strikethrough styles?

Slash Through uses U+0337, a diagonal overlay, for a more aggressive cross. X Crossed and Bracket Cross frame the struck text with symbols (✗ … ✗ or […]) for labels like 'sold out'. Pick the mood that fits your message.

Can I use strikethrough for sold out labels?

Yes. Marking items s̶o̶l̶d̶ o̶u̶t̶ without deleting them keeps the list readable and shows what changed. It also works for plain-text to-do lists: strike a task when it's done.

Does strikethrough text affect character limits?

Each combining mark is a character, so 'mistake' (7 letters) counts as 14 characters. Keep crossed phrases short on platforms with tight limits like bios.

Does strikethrough work on all phones?

Mostly. Some platforms strip combining marks as unusual characters, leaving plain text, and very old systems may render the line with small gaps. Modern phones display it correctly. Test on your audience's devices if it matters.

Strikethrough vs underline — when to use which?

Use strikethrough to retract, correct, or joke — the original stays readable under the line. Use underline to emphasize and draw the eye forward. They are opposite tools: one removes, the other highlights.
