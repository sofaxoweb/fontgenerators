Caption Length Counter

By FontGenerators Editorial Team · Last updated: October 12, 2026

A caption length counter is a free tool that counts the characters, words, lines, and emojis in your text and checks it against social media character limits. Paste any caption or bio and instantly see whether it fits Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X, and six more platforms — before you post and get cut off. No sign-up needed.

Key takeaways

  • Live counts for characters, words, lines, and emojis as you type or paste.
  • Checks your text against 10 platform limits, from TikTok’s 80-character bio to Facebook’s 63,206-character posts.
  • Each platform shows a clear fits / over-limit status with remaining characters.
  • Character counting follows JavaScript string length — most emoji count as 2 characters.
  • Free, instant, private: your text never leaves your browser.

What Is a Caption Length Counter?

A caption length counter is an online tool that measures your text and compares it against the character limits of major social platforms. You paste a caption, bio, or post; the tool reports characters, words, lines, and emoji counts, then shows a per-platform table with each limit and whether your text fits. This caption too long checker answers the “is my caption too long?” question in one glance — before Instagram truncates your bio mid-word or TikTok refuses your caption.

This social media limits checker — a dedicated caption character counter — is deliberately simple: no AI, no account, just counting. That makes it the fastest verification step in a posting workflow — write the caption in a generator, check it here, post with confidence.

How This Tool Works

  1. Paste or type. Any text goes into the input box — captions, bios, titles, posts. Counting starts with the first keystroke.
  2. Four live metrics. The tool computes: characters (JavaScript string length), words (split on whitespace), lines (split on line breaks), and emojis (matched against emoji Unicode ranges).
  3. Platform comparison. Your character count is checked against a built-in reference table of 10 platform limits (below), each shown with a fits/over status and characters remaining.
  4. Copy-safe editing. Trim in the same box and watch every platform status update live — no re-pasting into each app to test.

One technical note, stated plainly: the character count uses JavaScript’s .length, which counts UTF-16 code units. Most emoji therefore count as 2 characters here, and some complex emoji (flags, skin-tone sequences) count as more. Platforms count slightly differently from each other too, so treat tight margins (1–5 characters under a limit) with caution.

Why “Under the Limit” Is Not the Same as “Good Length”

Limits are ceilings, not targets — and the effective length is often far shorter. Instagram truncates captions at ~125 characters behind “more,” YouTube titles truncate around 60 characters in search, and TikTok bios get about two lines of screen space regardless of the 80-character allowance. So the useful workflow is two-step: use this counter to clear the hard limit, then edit toward the display length — hook in the first 125 characters of a caption, keyword in the first 60 of a title, the whole message in the first two lines of a bio. The counter tells you what fits; your eyes tell you what reads.

Emojis deserve special attention here because they are the most common surprise. A caption with 10 emoji can be 20+ characters “longer” than it looks, since most count as 2 characters each in this tool’s math (and platforms have their own counting quirks). If you are 3 characters under a limit and the text is emoji-heavy, give yourself a bigger buffer — or check the emoji count row and trim one.

Character Count vs. Word Count: Which Matters Where

Social platforms enforce character limits (the table above), while documents, essays, and ad platforms usually specify word counts — which is why this tool shows both. A 2,200-character Instagram caption is roughly 300–400 words; a 280-character X post is about 40–50. When a brief says “150-word bio,” nobody means Instagram’s 150-character bio — mixing the two up is the most common length mistake in social copy. This counter keeps both visible so the unit confusion cannot happen.

Limitations

  • Reference limits can go stale. Platforms change limits; the built-in table is a snapshot, not a live policy feed. If an app rejects text this tool calls fine, trust the app.
  • Counting methods differ. This tool counts like JavaScript; iOS, Android, and web apps each have quirks (especially with emoji sequences). Near-limit texts deserve a manual paste test.
  • No readability judgment. “Fits in 2,200 characters” says nothing about whether anyone will read it — pair with the formatter for presentation.
  • Ten platforms only. Niche platforms (Bluesky, Threads, Mastodon) are not in the reference table.
  • English-centric word splitting. Word counts split on whitespace, which is approximate for languages without spaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Instagram caption limit in 2026?

This tool’s built-in reference checks Instagram captions against a 2,200-character limit and Instagram bios against 150 characters. Paste your text above to check the instagram caption character limit instantly — the instagram caption limit 2026 in this tool’s reference is 2,200 characters — and note the display reality: only ~125 characters show before the “more” cutoff.

How many characters in a TikTok caption?

Against this counter’s reference table, TikTok captions are checked at 2,200 characters and TikTok bios at 80. In practice the strongest TikTok captions stay under 150 characters — short enough to read while the video plays.

How do I check if my caption is too long?

Paste it into the text box above. The tool counts characters live and marks each platform row fits or over-limit — find your target platform (for example Instagram → Caption) and trim until it turns green.

Do emojis count as characters?

Yes. This tool counts most emoji as 2 characters each (JavaScript string length), and complex emoji like flags can count as more. The dedicated emoji row shows your exact emoji count so you can see how much of the budget they consume.

What is the YouTube title character limit?

100 characters is the hard limit this tool checks — but aim for ~60, where search results truncate. The YouTube title generator shows per-title counts against the same limit.

What is the difference between character count and word count?

Character count includes every letter, space, and punctuation mark — it is what social platforms enforce. Word count groups characters into words — it is what essays and articles specify. This tool shows both so you never mix them up.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Counting happens entirely in your browser — your captions and bios never leave your device.

Why does another counter show a different character count?

Counting methods differ: some tools count emoji as 1 character, this one counts most as 2 (JavaScript length), and platforms each have their own quirks. For captions near a limit, the safest test is pasting into the actual app.

Does line-break spacing count toward the limit?

Yes — every line break and space is a character. Pretty multi-line captions cost characters; the line count row helps you see the tradeoff. Use the caption formatter to space captions efficiently.
