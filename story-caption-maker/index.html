Story Caption Maker

By FontGenio Editorial Team · Last updated: October 12, 2026

A story caption maker is a free tool that writes short captions for Instagram and TikTok stories from your story topic and story type. Enter what the story is about — “new haircut,” “weekend sale,” “morning coffee” — pick one of 6 types like poll, question, or countdown, and get copy-ready story texts instantly. No sign-up needed.

Key takeaways

  • Generates short story captions across 6 types: engagement, poll, question, CTA, mood, and countdown.
  • Each type holds 8 templates — 48 total — with your topic filled in.
  • Built for 24-hour story content: stickers, polls, and swipe-ups pair with every caption — all outputs are short story texts, one to two lines.
  • Every caption shows its character count with one-click copy.
  • Free, instant, private: everything runs in your browser.

What Is a Story Caption Maker?

A story caption maker is an online tool that creates short caption texts for Instagram Stories and TikTok stories. You enter the story’s topic and choose the story type; the tool fills interaction-focused templates with your topic and returns options to copy and paste. Stories are a different medium from feed posts — vertical, ephemeral, tappable — so these captions are written short, conversational, and paired with actions: vote, reply, tap, swipe.

This IG story ideas tool is template-based and instant. It does not write your life story; it writes the five words that get the tap — the poll prompt, the countdown line, the question sticker text — which is exactly what story captions are for.

How This Tool Works

  1. Story topic input. A few words on what the story shows. The topic fills the {topic} placeholder in each template.
  2. Story type selection. Six buttons — engagement, poll, question, CTA (call-to-action), mood, countdown — set the caption’s job. Engagement is the default.
  3. Template fill. Each type holds 8 short templates. The tool shuffles them, picks a set, and inserts your topic.
  4. Character count. Every caption displays its length — story text boxes are small, so short wins.
  5. One-click copy. Copy any caption and paste it into your story’s text tool or sticker.

Regenerating reshuffles the templates, and switching types changes the caption’s entire job — from driving replies (question) to driving urgency (countdown).

Worked Example: Real Input, Real Output

We ran the tool with the topic “new haircut” and the poll story type. Three captions it actually returned:

  • “Poll: new haircut - Yes or No? 🤔”
  • “This or That: new haircut edition 👀”
  • “Rate my new haircut: 1-10 ⭐”

These are verbatim outputs — notice the pattern: every poll caption ends with the mechanism (Yes/No, A/B, 1-10 scale) because a poll caption without a voting instruction gets scrolled past. Pair any of these with Instagram’s actual poll sticker and the caption + sticker do the same job twice, which is the point: redundancy across text and sticker catches both readers and tappers.

How to Use This Story Caption Tool

  1. Type your story topic (flash sale, gym selfie, new blog post).
  2. Pick the story type matching your goal — poll for opinions, CTA for link taps, countdown for launches.
  3. Click Generate and choose the shortest option that says it all.
  4. Copy it and paste into your story text — then add the matching sticker (poll, question, countdown).
  5. Keep on-screen text under ~3 lines so it never covers the photo.

Pair Every Caption With Its Sticker

Story captions fail when the text and the sticker ask for different things — a poll caption over a link sticker, a question caption with no question sticker. The six types map directly onto Instagram and TikTok’s interactive stickers: poll captions go with the poll sticker, question with the question box, countdown with the countdown sticker, CTA with the link sticker. Generate the caption first, then add the sticker that completes its sentence. Viewers who read the text tap the sticker; viewers who only see the sticker still get the gist — that redundancy is what lifts story interactions, which is the metric that keeps your stories at the front of the tray.

Placement matters as much as pairing. Story text competes with the photo, the progress bar, and the reply field — keep captions to the upper or middle third, under three lines, in high-contrast color. A perfect caption hidden behind the reply box may as well not exist.

Urgency Is the Story Format’s Superpower

Stories disappear in 24 hours, and the captions that perform best lean into that instead of fighting it. The countdown type exists for exactly this: “48 hours left,” “doors close tonight” — deadlines that are real because the format enforces them. Even non-sales stories benefit: “answering these for the next hour” (question type) or “voting ends tonight” (poll type) manufactures the now-or-never that feed posts cannot. The rule: if a story caption would work identically as a feed caption, it is not using the format — add the time element and watch replies climb.

Limitations

  • Text only — no sticker logic. The tool writes the words; you still add the actual poll/question/countdown sticker in the app.
  • Very short by design. These are one-to-two-line captions. Anything narrative belongs in a feed post — see the Instagram caption generator.
  • English only. All 48 templates are written in English.
  • Generic urgency. Countdown templates say “soon” and “tonight” — fill in your real deadline before posting, or the urgency reads hollow.
  • Repeats on regenerate. Eight templates per type means frequent regenerating resurfaces captions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best Instagram story captions?

The best Instagram story captions are under three lines, ask for one specific action (vote, reply, tap), and pair with the matching sticker. Pick the story type above that matches your goal — poll, question, CTA, mood, or countdown — and keep the text short enough to read in two seconds.

Where can I find short captions for stories?

Enter your story topic above, choose a type, and generate — every caption is written short for the story format. The mood type gives one-line vibe captions; engagement gives action-driving lines.

What are good IG story ideas?

Polls (“this or that”), question boxes (AMAs, recommendations), countdowns (launches, events), and behind-the-scenes clips consistently earn taps. This tool’s six types map to exactly those formats — generate a caption for the format you are posting.

How long should a story caption be?

One to three short lines. Story viewers decide in about two seconds, and long text blocks cover the photo — defeating the purpose. Every caption this tool generates shows its character count so you can pick the shortest strong option.

Should story captions match the sticker?

Yes — always. A poll caption needs the poll sticker, a question caption needs the question box, a countdown caption needs the countdown sticker. Mismatched text and stickers confuse viewers and kill the tap you were after.

Can I use these captions on TikTok stories?

Yes. The captions are platform-neutral short texts — paste them into TikTok stories the same way. For full TikTok videos (not stories), use the TikTok caption generator instead.

Do stories need hashtags?

One location or niche hashtag can help discovery, but stories are primarily shown to existing followers — hashtags matter far less than in feed posts. If you add one, shrink it small and tuck it in a corner rather than letting it dominate the caption.

Is the story caption maker free?

Completely free with no account — generate and copy as many story captions as you like. Your topics never leave your browser.

What is the difference between this and the Instagram caption generator?

Format and lifespan. This tool writes short, interactive captions for 24-hour stories (polls, questions, countdowns). The Instagram caption generator writes fuller captions for permanent feed posts and Reels. Use each where the content lives.
