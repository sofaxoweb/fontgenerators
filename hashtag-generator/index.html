What Are Hashtags and How Do They Work?

A hashtag is a word or phrase preceded by the # symbol that categorizes content and makes it discoverable. On Instagram and TikTok, hashtags are indexed in real-time and used as search terms. When you post with a hashtag, your content appears in that hashtag's explore feed. The discoverability value of a hashtag depends on its size: very popular hashtags (10M+ posts) have fierce competition; small niche hashtags (5K–100K) have a better chance of ranking but less total traffic. The optimal strategy combines all three tiers — large, medium, and niche — for maximum reach and relevance.

Why Hashtag Strategy Matters

Randomly adding popular hashtags is one of the most common mistakes on social media. #Love has 2 billion+ posts — your content will never be seen there. #MorningCoffeeLover has 50,000 posts — you can rank in the top posts for a week and get genuine discovery. The goal is to be a big fish in a mid-size pond. Strategic hashtag selection also signals to the algorithm what topic your content belongs to, which improves distribution to interested audiences even beyond hashtag search — through the Explore page and For You feed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use on Instagram in 2024?

Instagram officially recommends 3–5 relevant hashtags per post. While you can use up to 30, the algorithm now deprioritizes posts that look like hashtag spam. Quality and relevance beat quantity.

Do hashtags still work on TikTok?

Yes, but differently. TikTok's algorithm weighs content signals (watch time, completion rate, shares) more heavily than hashtags. Still, 3–5 relevant hashtags improve discoverability, especially in search. Include at least one niche hashtag.

Should I use the same hashtags on every post?

No. Using identical hashtags on every post trains Instagram to mark them as spam. Rotate between 3–4 different hashtag sets that are relevant to your different content categories.

What are banned hashtags and how do I avoid them?

Instagram bans hashtags associated with spam, adult content, or policy violations. Banned hashtags suppress the entire post's reach. Check if a hashtag is banned by searching it on Instagram — if the 'Recent Posts' tab is missing, it's likely banned or limited.

Do hashtags work in Instagram comments?

Yes, adding hashtags in the comments (within the first minute of posting) works the same as hashtags in the caption. This is a common trick to keep captions clean while still using hashtags for discoverability.
