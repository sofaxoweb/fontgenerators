Line Break Fixer — Fix Line Breaks

Paste text from a PDF or web page and every line often breaks in the wrong place — sentences chopped mid-thought, paragraphs shattered into fragments. This free line break fixer repairs broken lines in five ways: join broken lines into paragraphs, collapse everything to one line, add paragraph spacing, number lines, or strip empty lines. Paste your text, pick a mode, and get clean paragraphs.

Key takeaways

  • Five modes: join broken lines, single line, double spacing, add line numbers, remove empty lines.
  • "Join broken lines" merges single line breaks but preserves blank-line paragraph breaks — ideal for PDF pastes.
  • "Single line" collapses everything (including paragraphs) into one trimmed line.
  • "Add line numbers" numbers every line, including blank ones.
  • Runs entirely in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

What Is a Line Break Fixer?

A line break fixer is an online tool that repairs unwanted line breaks in text. When you copy from PDFs, e-books, web pages, or chat apps, text often arrives with a hard line break at the end of every visual line — splitting sentences and destroying paragraph structure. A paragraph formatter like this one re-joins those fragments, removes extra blank lines, or reformats the spacing so the text reads as proper paragraphs again.

[IMAGE: Before/after diagram showing PDF-copied text with broken mid-sentence line breaks repaired into flowing paragraphs — alt: "Broken line breaks from a PDF paste repaired into clean paragraphs"]

How This Line Break Fixer Works

Choose one of five modes; the fix applies to the output box instantly (or click "Fix Line Breaks"):

  • Join broken lines (default) — single line breaks become spaces, so wrapped lines rejoin into flowing sentences. Blank lines are preserved, so paragraph breaks survive. This is the mode for PDF copy-paste.
  • Single line — every line break becomes a space and the whole text collapses into one trimmed line. Paragraph structure is discarded.
  • Double spacing — empty lines are dropped, then the remaining lines are joined with a blank line between each, giving evenly spaced paragraphs.
  • Add line numbers — every line gets a number ("1. ", "2. ", ...), including blank lines.
  • Remove empty lines — whitespace-only lines are deleted; everything else stays exactly as-is.

Copy Result copies the fixed text. Clear empties both boxes.

Worked Example

A poem pasted with hard breaks:

Roses are red,
violets are blue.

Sugar is sweet,
and so are you.

Join broken lines produces:

Roses are red, violets are blue.

Sugar is sweet, and so are you.

The two verse lines joined into sentences, while the blank line between stanzas survived — the tool only merges single breaks. Single line mode on the same input gives one line: Roses are red, violets are blue. Sugar is sweet, and so are you.

How to Use the Line Break Fixer

  1. Paste the broken text into the input box.
  2. Pick a mode: "Join broken lines" for PDF/web pastes, "Remove empty lines" for gappy text, "Single line" for one-line fields.
  3. Review the result — the output updates as soon as you switch modes.
  4. Copy Result and paste the fixed text into your document.

Join Broken Lines vs Single Line: Which Mode?

The two most-used modes solve different problems:

  • Use "join broken lines" when the text has real paragraph structure you want to keep — articles, essays, e-book chapters. It heals wrapped lines but respects blank-line paragraph breaks.
  • Use "single line" when structure must go entirely: pasting into a single-line form field, a spreadsheet cell, a tweet composer, or an AI prompt box. Everything becomes one trimmed line.

A common workflow: join broken lines first to restore paragraphs, then if you need single-line output, switch to single-line mode — switching modes re-processes from your original input each time. When the structure is fixed, verify the final length with the word counter.

Why PDF Copy-Paste Breaks Lines

PDFs store text as positioned glyphs on a page, not as flowing paragraphs. When you copy, the clipboard receives a line break wherever the visual line ended — often mid-sentence — plus hyphenation artifacts ("docu-\nment"). The join mode was designed for exactly this: it merges those visual-line breaks back into spaces. What it cannot fix automatically:

  • Hyphenated word splits across lines ("docu- ment") — remove the hyphen manually or with find-and-replace.
  • Headers, footers, and page numbers copied inline — delete them before or after fixing.
  • Two-column layouts, which interleave the columns line by line — those need manual reordering.
[IMAGE: Diagram explaining why PDFs insert line breaks at visual line endings when copying text — alt: "Why copying from a PDF creates broken line breaks mid-sentence"]

Limitations

  • "Add line numbers" numbers blank lines too — run "Remove empty lines" first if you want continuous numbering of content lines only.
  • Hyphenated line-breaks are not rejoined ("docu-\nment" keeps its hyphen).
  • No smart paragraph detection: "join" trusts blank lines as paragraph breaks; if your source has none, use "double spacing" after joining to rebuild them.
  • Whitespace inside lines is untouched except in single-line mode; for spacing cleanup use the text cleaner.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fix broken line breaks from a PDF?

Paste the text and use the default 'Join broken lines' mode: it turns single line breaks into spaces (rejoining wrapped sentences) while preserving blank lines as paragraph breaks. Then manually delete any headers, page numbers, or hyphen splits the PDF introduced.

How do I remove line breaks from text completely?

Choose the 'Single line' mode. Every line break becomes a space and the entire text collapses into one trimmed line — ideal for single-line form fields, spreadsheet cells, or prompt boxes.

What is the difference between 'join broken lines' and 'single line'?

'Join broken lines' merges only single line breaks and keeps blank-line paragraph structure. 'Single line' discards all structure and collapses everything into one line. Use join for articles and essays; single line for one-line destinations.

How do I add line numbers to text?

Select the 'Add line numbers' mode — every line is prefixed with its number. Note that blank lines are numbered too; run 'Remove empty lines' first if you want only content lines numbered.

How do I remove extra blank lines?

The 'Remove empty lines' mode deletes every whitespace-only line while leaving all other lines untouched — the fastest way to remove extra line breaks without joining anything. For evenly spaced paragraphs instead, use 'Double spacing'.

Why does copied PDF text have line breaks mid-sentence?

PDFs store positioned glyphs rather than flowing paragraphs, so copying inserts a break at each visual line ending. This tool's join mode reverses exactly that damage.

Can I format text into proper paragraphs?

Yes: 'Join broken lines' restores paragraph flow from wrapped lines, and 'Double spacing' rebuilds even blank-line separation between paragraphs after dropping empty lines.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All five modes run in JavaScript in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Will it fix hyphenated words split across lines?

No — 'docu-\nment' keeps its hyphen after joining. Remove those hyphens with find-and-replace (search '- ' at line ends) before or after fixing.
